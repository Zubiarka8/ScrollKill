## The short version

Scroll Kill does not collect your data, does not send anything off your device, and has
no servers, no account system, and no analytics or advertising. Everything the app
records stays on your phone.

## What the app does

Scroll Kill helps you reduce compulsive use of infinite-scroll feeds. With your
consent, it uses Android's AccessibilityService to recognise when an infinite feed
(such as Reels, Shorts, or a For You page) is open in a social app you have chosen to
watch, so it can nudge you to stop once you have reached a daily time limit you set.

## What Scroll Kill reads

While you have enabled the accessibility service, Scroll Kill reads on-screen text and
layout information from the specific apps you choose to watch, only while they are in
the foreground. This information is used on your device, in the moment, only to decide
whether an infinite feed is on screen. It is not saved, not written to logs in the
released app, and cannot be sent anywhere - the app has no internet permission.

## What Scroll Kill stores on your device

- **Usage history:** for each watched session, the app package name, which feed was
  detected, start and end times, duration, and counts of detections and nudges. This is
  kept in a local database to show you your usage and to enforce your daily limits. You
  can choose how long this history is kept, in Settings.
- **Your settings:** which apps you watch, your daily limits, and other preferences.

None of this is derived from the text on your screen, and none of it leaves your
device. Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of Scroll Kill's data are disabled.

## What Scroll Kill does not do

- No data collection or transmission of any kind.
- No user accounts, sign-in, or cloud processing.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs of those kinds.
- No selling or sharing of data - there is no data to sell or share.

## Permissions

Scroll Kill requests no Android runtime permissions. It uses one special access,
**Accessibility**, which you grant explicitly in Android Settings and can revoke at any
time. Turning it off stops all detection.

## Your control

- Turn the accessibility service off at any time in Android Settings.
- Change or remove which apps are watched, and your limits, in the app's Settings.
- Shorten how long usage history is retained in Settings.
- Uninstalling the app, or using "Clear storage" in Android's app settings, permanently
  deletes all of Scroll Kill's local data.

## Children

Scroll Kill is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children.
